Formula CBAM Australia (Proposed – Safeguard Mechanism Mirror)

CBAM Liability = (EI_import − Benchmark_SM(t)) × Volume × Carbon_Price_Gap
Benchmark_SM(t) = EI_base_2024 × (1 − 0.049)t  ← ERC (Emissions Reduction Contribution)
Benchmark 2024: Klinker = 841 kgCO₂e/t · Semen = 708 kgCO₂e/t · Penurunan 4,9%/tahun berlaku mulai 2025 · Carbon Price Gap = Harga karbon Australia − Biaya karbon negara asal
